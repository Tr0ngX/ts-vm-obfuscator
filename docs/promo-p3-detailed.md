## Phân Tách Từng Yếu Tố

### 1. Composition & Camera

| Yếu tố | Chi tiết |
|---|---|
| **Frame** | 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) |
| **Shot type** | Close-up single shield → Pull-back to wide 3 shields |
| **Camera movement** | Slow zoom out from center shield, slight upward tilt |
| **Depth of field** | Shallow on center shield, other 2 blurry → deep focus khi full reveal |
